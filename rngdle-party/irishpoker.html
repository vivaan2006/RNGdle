How it plays

  1. The deal. Four face-down cards each. Guess your next card, four rounds: red/black, higher/lower, inside/outside, then the suit. Right = give sips. Wrong = drink. Hit the post = drink double.
  2. Memorize. Your hand flips face-down for the rest of the game.
  3. Give & Take. Eight cards flip one by one. Take cards: holders drink. Give cards: claim you've got one and point at someone — they drink, or call bluff and the loser drinks double.
  4. Ride the bus. Whoever matched the most rides — no way out. Call higher/lower through the row; any miss means drink and start over.
